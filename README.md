# 🌐 My Personal Portfolio Website

## 📌 About the Project

Welcome to my very first GitHub repository!

This repository contains the basic framework of my **Personal Portfolio Website**, developed using fundamental HTML (HyperText Markup Language). As my initial step into web development, I have created a simple and structured portfolio to introduce myself, showcase my academic journey, highlight my acquired skills and experiences, and provide an overview of my future interests and career aspirations.

The website is built entirely with **basic HTML**, without incorporating any additional frontend technologies or frameworks such as CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, or other libraries. The primary objective of this project is to understand the fundamentals of HTML, webpage structuring, hyperlinks, navigation, and the organization of website content.

## 🎯 Objectives of the Project

The main purpose of developing this portfolio is to:

- Create a personal digital space to introduce myself and my background.
- Present my schooling journey and academic qualifications.
- Showcase the technical and interpersonal skills I have acquired.
- Highlight my learning experiences, achievements, and areas of interest.
- Introduce my future-oriented domains and career aspirations.
- Provide accessible links through which visitors, recruiters, peers, and professionals can connect with me.

## 📂 Website Overview

The portfolio is organized into different sections to make the information easy to navigate and understand.

### 1. About Me
A brief introduction highlighting my personality, academic background, interests, learning mindset, and professional aspirations.

### 2. Education
An overview of my educational journey, including my schooling experience, academic qualifications, and achievements.

### 3. Skills
A dedicated section presenting the technical and interpersonal skills I have acquired and continue to develop.

### 4. Experience & Learning
A space to highlight my learning experiences, participation, exposure to different activities, and practical involvement in technology-related domains.

### 5. Future Interests & Career Aspirations
An introduction to the domains I aspire to explore and develop my expertise in, particularly Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

### 6. Connect With Me
A dedicated section containing relevant social and professional links, allowing visitors to connect with me and explore my professional presence.
